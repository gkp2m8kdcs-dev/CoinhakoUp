Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how CoinhakoUp ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our mobile application. By using CoinhakoUp, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

CoinhakoUp does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with us or with any third party. The app is designed to operate entirely on your device.

Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number, address)
  • Account credentials or authentication data
  • Financial account information, exchange API keys, or wallet addresses
  • Location data or device identifiers for tracking
  • Usage analytics, telemetry, or behavioral data
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA)
  • Contact lists, photos, or other personal content from your device

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

CoinhakoUp stores all your journal entries, transaction history, holdings, watchlist, and settings locally on your iPhone in a private JSON vault within the app's sandboxed storage. This data:

  • Never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it
  • Is not backed up to our servers (only to your iCloud backup, if you have iCloud Backup enabled in iOS settings)
  • Can be deleted at any time via the "Reset All Data" option in Settings
  • Is removed automatically when you delete the app

You may also export your journal history as a CSV file using the in-app export feature. That export is generated and saved by you; we never see it.

3. No Network Communication

CoinhakoUp does not require an internet connection to function. The app does not make outbound requests to our servers or to any third-party service for tracking, analytics, advertising, or remote configuration. Market prices and coin information shown in the app are bundled with the app and refreshed locally.

4. No Third-Party Services

CoinhakoUp does not integrate any third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising, social media, or crash reporting. There are no cookies, no tracking pixels, and no cross-app identifiers.

5. Children's Privacy

CoinhakoUp is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect any information from children. Because no information is collected at all, this policy applies universally.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Because all data is local to your device, you have full control at all times:

  • Access: View all your data directly within the app
  • Export: Generate a CSV export of your journal at any time
  • Delete: Use "Reset All Data" or delete the app to remove everything

7. Data Security

Your data benefits from iOS's built-in app sandbox and file-system protections. Because data never leaves your device, the risk of remote data exposure is eliminated. You are responsible for safeguarding access to your device itself (e.g., using a passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID).

8. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy (for example, if a future feature requires data collection), we will update this page and, where appropriate, notify you through the app before the change takes effect.
