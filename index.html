What is CoinhakoUp?

CoinhakoUp is a private, on-device investment journal designed for the long-term crypto investor who wants to think clearly about positions, sizing, and risk. It is a tracker and analyzer — not a trading platform.

  • Scan the top cryptocurrencies with sparkline previews and sortable markets
  • Log every transaction independently — buy, sell, or receive
  • Track total value, daily change, realized and unrealized P&L
  • Build an interactive performance curve and allocation donut
  • Use tools: converter, DCA simulator, scenario planner, rebalancer, heatmap
  • Export your journal history as CSV for your accountant
  • Everything stored locally in a JSON vault on your iPhone

Frequently Asked Questions

Does CoinhakoUp connect to my exchange?

No. CoinhakoUp never connects to exchanges, never places orders, and never custodies assets. Every entry you log represents a transaction you made independently.

Where is my data stored?

All your data is stored locally on your device in a JSON vault. Nothing is sent to a server. Reset All Data is one tap away in Settings.

Do I need an account?

No account, no sign-in, no telemetry. You simply install the app and start journaling.

Is this investment advice?

No. CoinhakoUp provides educational content only. It is not investment, tax, or legal advice. Crypto is volatile — you may lose all capital allocated.
